# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual
identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best for the community as a whole

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- The use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## One addition specific to this project

**Do not overstate what the software does.** This is terminology software for
clinical use, and a wrong terminology answer is invisible in a way a crash is
not — [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) treats an incorrect subsumption or ECL
result as the most serious class of defect for exactly that reason, and
[`AI_STATEMENT.md`](AI_STATEMENT.md) §12 names the ways this project is most
likely to be wrong. Asserting in an issue, a pull request, a review, or a
public post that this software does something it has not been shown to do is
treated here as a conduct problem as well as a technical one, because someone
may act on it.

Correcting such a claim — including one made by the maintainer — is welcome,
and is not a hostile act. Say what you did not verify; the bar is the same for
everyone, and [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) ranks exactly this kind of
correction as the most valuable contribution this project can receive.

## Enforcement responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards
and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any
behaviour they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to
this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when
appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

Where participation happens in someone else's space — notably SNOMED
International's own community channels and document sites — that community's
own code of conduct and intellectual-property rules govern, and this one does
not override them. In particular, nothing here licenses anyone to move SNOMED
CT release content through this project's issue tracker; that is barred by
[`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) rule 2 regardless of intent.

## Enforcement

Report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour to
[joel@joelparkerhenderson.com](mailto:joel@joelparkerhenderson.com). All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of
any incident. Note that this project has a single maintainer
([`MAINTAINERS.md`](MAINTAINERS.md)); if your report concerns that person, you
have no internal escalation path, and that limitation is stated here rather than
hidden.
